# Khachbar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · Samvel Matinyan

Khachbar does not require an account and never asks for your name or email.

## What we collect

- Anonymous usage and crash data (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics), to improve the app.
- A notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), only if you allow notifications.
- Advertising data such as device info and IP address (Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads). Your Advertising Identifier is used only if you allow tracking.

Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. We do not sell your data. These providers' own policies also apply: [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Meta](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy), [Unity](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy), [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Your choices

You can turn off tracking or notifications in iOS Settings at any time, or delete the app. For any other request, email us.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
